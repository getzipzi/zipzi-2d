# Core-only benchmark — 2026-09-17

This run links the same C++ harness directly against each Cubism Core C ABI
provider. It does not use Cubism Framework, Godot, a graphics API, texture
loading, motion evaluation, or physics. The fixture is Nijiiro Mao
(`mao_pro.moc3`, 870,272 bytes): 128 parameters, 31 parts, 260 drawables, and
6,012 vertices. Each steady-state result is the median of three 750 ms trials.
Trials run in alternating provider order and the working-set phase contains 40
independent model instances.

Host: Apple M4 Mac mini (`Mac16,10`), arm64, macOS 27.0 build 26A428.
Repository: `fa8ee78`; PurismCore: `350fe62`.

## Steady-state median

Lower latency is better. “Relative” is throughput relative to official Cubism
Core for the same phase.

| Phase | Official Cubism | Ayagami | Purism | Ayagami relative | Purism relative |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Parameter write (128 parameters) | 77 ns | 76 ns | 76 ns | 1.01x | 1.00x |
| Part-opacity write (31 parts) | 13 ns | 13 ns | 13 ns | 0.98x | 1.00x |
| Idle model update | 42.05 us | 7.79 us | 34.69 us | 5.40x | 1.21x |
| Changed-input model update | 47.60 us | 119.34 us | 39.82 us | 0.40x | 1.20x |
| Dynamic drawable/part readback | 4.30 us | 4.19 us | 4.21 us | 1.02x | 1.02x |
| Static metadata/index readback | 22.58 us | 22.05 us | 21.94 us | 1.02x | 1.03x |
| 40-model input write + update + dynamic readback | 59.40 us/model | 134.09 us/model | 46.79 us/model | 0.44x | 1.27x |

The full working-set throughput was 16,836 models/s for official Cubism,
7,458 models/s for Ayagami, and 21,370 models/s for Purism.

## Startup median

These figures use 17 bounded samples. Copy time is shown because consistency
and revive each operate on a fresh copy of the moc bytes. The public ABI has no
destroy function, so startup is intentionally not an unbounded throughput loop.

| Operation | Official Cubism | Ayagami | Purism |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Copy 870 KB moc | 12.50 us | 11.96 us | 12.08 us |
| Consistency check including copy | 21.21 us | 113.25 us | 24.75 us |
| Revive including copy | 16.21 us | 201.13 us | 30.21 us |
| Initialize model | 42.38 us | 188.88 us | 40.58 us |

## Interpretation

Purism is consistently faster than official Cubism in the actual deformation
update: about 20% with changing inputs and 21% when inputs are unchanged. Once
parameter and part-opacity changes plus realistic drawable traversal are
included across a 40-model working set, the end-to-end advantage is about 27%.
That is material, but it is not an order-of-magnitude pure-compute win.

Ayagami's dirty/idle path is exceptionally fast, but its changed-parameter
update is about 2.51x slower than official Cubism and its 40-model pipeline is
about 2.26x slower. Its startup paths are also substantially slower. The three
providers report identical structural counts and canvas metadata; checksums are
finite but are not expected to be bit-identical across independent Core
implementations.

Raw repeated results are generated locally at
`benchmarks/cubism-matrix/artifacts/results/latest-core-only.json` by:

```sh
python3 benchmarks/cubism-matrix/tools/matrix.py benchmark-core --repeats 3
```
